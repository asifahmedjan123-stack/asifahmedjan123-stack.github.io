Ad Runner
Tools for local businesses

One app for your business advertising

Ad Runner is an Android app being developed to help local businesses and shops in Pakistan prepare, create and manage advertising from one place.

What Ad Runner is designed to do

  • Connect advertising accounts with your permission.
  • Prepare campaigns for Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp-click ads, TikTok and YouTube through the Google Ads API.
  • Manage campaign settings and review advertising performance.

Platform integrations are subject to provider approval, account eligibility and availability. This website is informational; it does not launch campaigns, accept payments or provide an app download.

You stay in control

Review your account connections and campaign settings before authorizing advertising. Advertising platforms set their own charges and policies. Ad Runner is independently operated and is not endorsed by Google, Meta or TikTok.
