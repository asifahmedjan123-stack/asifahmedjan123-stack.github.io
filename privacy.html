Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This policy describes Ad Runner, an Android advertising-management app operated by Muhammad Asif in Karachi, Pakistan. Contact: nazumar607@gmail.com. Integrations described below apply only when available and when you authorize them.

Information and purposes

  • Google sign-in: with your permission, account identifiers, name, email address and profile image may be used to identify you and connect your account.
  • Google Ads API: authorized advertising account identifiers, account details, campaigns, ads, assets, budgets and performance reports are used only to provide the advertising features you request. Access does not authorize us to run unrelated campaigns.
  • Meta/Facebook login: the profile identifiers and other permissions you approve, such as access to selected Pages, business or advertising accounts, are used to connect and manage the selected accounts. Instagram and WhatsApp-click advertising use the relevant authorized Meta advertising features; this does not grant access to private WhatsApp messages.
  • Other platform connections: TikTok account and advertising data are used only for the functions you authorize, when that integration is available.
  • Your input: business descriptions, ad text, images, videos, targeting selections and campaign settings are used to prepare and manage your campaigns.
  • Support: messages and the details you choose to email us are used to respond to your request.

Storage and retention

Depending on the enabled feature, the app may retain account connection identifiers, authorization tokens, campaign drafts, selected advertising settings and cached reports on your device or in the service supporting that feature. Advertising platforms also store the data sent to them under their own policies. We do not request your Google, Facebook or TikTok password.

We retain data only for the connected features, support, security and any applicable legal obligations. Disconnecting an account stops future access but may not delete campaigns or records already held by an advertising platform. You can request deletion of data under our control by email. Any records that must legally be retained are restricted to that purpose.

Sharing and no sale

We do not sell or rent personal information or Google user data. Data is sent to the platform you choose when needed to carry out your instructions. Service providers may process only the information necessary to operate and secure the service under appropriate restrictions. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or with your explicit consent. Your Google account data is not shared with Meta or TikTok for their independent use.

Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use

Ad Runner’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is used only to provide or improve the user-facing features you request. It is not used for advertising targeting unrelated to those features, sold to data brokers, used for creditworthiness decisions, or used to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. Humans do not read Google user data except with your affirmative agreement for specific information, when necessary for security, to comply with law, or for permitted internal operations using aggregated and anonymized data.

Your choices and deletion

You may revoke access in your Google Account’s third-party connections settings or the corresponding Meta/TikTok settings. To request access, correction or deletion of Ad Runner data, email nazumar607@gmail.com with the subject “Ad Runner data deletion” and the email or account identifier needed to locate your records. Never send passwords, authorization tokens or payment details. We may ask for reasonable ownership confirmation, acknowledge your request and explain any legally required retention. Separately delete platform-held campaigns through that platform.

Security, children and website

We use reasonable access and security precautions; no system is completely secure. Ad Runner is intended for adult business users, not children under 18. This static website has no analytics, advertising cookies or external trackers. A hosting provider may process normal request logs for delivery and security.

Changes and contact

Updates will be published here with a revised effective date. For questions, contact Muhammad Asif at nazumar607@gmail.com.
